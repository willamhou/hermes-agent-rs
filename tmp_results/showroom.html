28TH PET FAIR ASIA

Pet Fair Asia 2026 is set to welcome an unprecedented number of exhibitors and visitors from China and around the globe as it returns to Shanghai from August 19–23, 2026 for its 28th edition. The largest exhibition for the pet industry in Asia-Pacific continues its strong growth trajectory, promising another record-breaking year in scale, innovation, and international participation. More than 2,600 exhibitors are expected to showcase the latest advancements in pet care, products, and services across 320,000+ square meters of gross exhibition space, surpassing the scale of last year' s milestone event.
